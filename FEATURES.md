# Taskito — Feature Brief

Taskito is a personal productivity app that turns everyday tasks into a daily habit. Showing up counts: saving a check-in, adding a task, or completing one keeps your streak alive.

## Core Features

- **Tasks** — Today's list with progress bar and All/Active/Done filters. Task fields: title, notes, priority, category, due date, start time, duration, repeat, reminder, streak tracking. Simple and Advanced modes, quick add with `Shift + A`, friendly toasts and empty states.
- **Task Groups** — Private named groups (e.g. Work, Home) to organize related tasks.
- **Streaks** — Daily streak for overall activity, task-specific streaks, best streak, streak calendar, 30-day trend chart, and weekly activity stats.
- **Days & Mood Check-ins** — Daily journal with five moods (Awful → Great), notes, Up/Down day ratings, monthly mood summary, mood charts, and recent check-ins.
- **Friends** — Friend requests (send, accept, decline, cancel, remove), search, suggested people, and public profiles limited to name, username, bio, and streaks.
- **Challenges** — 21/30/75/100-day community challenges across Fitness, Mind, Discipline, Productivity, and Money. Create your own, join to get a daily task, share via native share or link.
- **Leaderboards** — Friends and global rankings over a rolling 30 days, podium for the top three, per-challenge rankings, and a score out of 100 based on completion (50%), consistency (30%), and on-time rate (20%).
- **Notifications** — Friend request updates with unread badge, mark one/all as read, delete, and deep links to the requests dialog.
- **Profile** — Identity card, activity stats, leaderboard score ring, shortcuts, six achievements, and log out.
- **Analytics** — 7/14/30-day range with KPIs (completed tasks, completion rate, focus time, best streak) and charts for activity, categories, mood, up/down days, focus by hour, and weekly comparison.
- **Settings** — Edit profile, change password, light/dark/system theme, notification preferences, and clear all app data with a typed `DELETE` confirmation.
- **Help & Support** — Expandable FAQs and quick links to key pages.
- **Accounts** — Register with email verification, log in, forgot/reset password, and protected private routes.

## Experience

- Three themes (light, dark, system) with a calm blue accent and Space Grotesk typography.
- Mobile-first layout with a floating bottom navigation bar for Tasks, Streaks, Days, and Friends.
- Motion and feedback: page transitions, staggered entrances, toasts, confirmation dialogs, skeleton loading states, and reduced-motion support.
- Accessibility: visible focus rings, aria labels and states, keyboard navigation, labeled forms, and text alternatives for charts.
- Privacy: all data is per-account; friends never see email, tasks, or notes; challenge visibility is opt-in.

## Not Yet Available

- Accent color customization (preview only).
- Push or email delivery of reminders and notification preferences.
- Separate challenge check-in screen (progress comes from the joined task).
- Direct messaging or an activity feed.
